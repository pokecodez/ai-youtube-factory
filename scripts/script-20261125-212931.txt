**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:** 
*A brooding, cinematic drone shot of the remote, wave-lashed Flannan Isles in Scotland under a grey, overcast sky.*

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a supply ship reached the remote Flannan Isles lighthouse off the coast of Scotland. What the crew found inside still chills the spine. The heavy iron entrance gate was locked. Inside, the kitchen fire was cold, a meal remained untouched on the table, and two of the three waterproof coats were missing. Yet, a ferocious storm had battered the islands days earlier, and experienced keepers would never leave their post unprotected. The logbook recorded eerie entries: panicked prayers and weeping during the gale, despite no storms being reported in the area. The three men vanished without a trace, leaving behind an island shrouded in eternal silence.

**Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown**

*   **Verified Facts:** 
    *   In December 1900, the relief ship *Hesperus* arrived at the Eilean Mor lighthouse in the Flannan Isles and found it unmanned.
    *   The entrance gate was locked, the kitchen fire was unlit, a meal was left on the table, and two of the three standard oilskins (coats) were missing. 
    *   The station logbook contained unusual entries regarding severe weather and distress from the keepers in the days leading up to their disappearance, despite local weather reports not matching the intensity described.
    *   Extensive searches yielded no bodies or definitive clues as to the men's fate.

*   **Speculation:** 
    *   The exact cause of the disappearance remains unknown. Popular speculations—ranging from a rogue giant wave sweeping them off the cliffs while securing equipment, to falling down a cliffside, to more sensational theories like alien abduction, sea monsters, or foul play—are entirely unproven.
