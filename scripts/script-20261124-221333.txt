### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
*   In December 1900, the relief ship *Hesperus* arrived at the Eilean Mor lighthouse in the Flannan Isles and found the station deserted.
*   The three keepers (James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur) were missing, and no trace of them was ever found.
*   The station's logbooks showed entries up to December 15, 1900.
*   Inside the lighthouse, two oilskin coats were left behind, while a third keeper's coat was missing. 
*   The official inquiry concluded that the men were likely swept out to sea by a sudden, massive wave while tending to equipment during severe weather.

**Speculation:**
*   Popular folklore and sensationalized accounts often attribute the disappearances to sea monsters, foreign spies, ghost ships, or sudden madness/abduction. 
*   The exact mechanics of how all three men supposedly ended up in the water simultaneously against standard safety protocols remain a subject of debate among historians and maritime enthusiasts.
