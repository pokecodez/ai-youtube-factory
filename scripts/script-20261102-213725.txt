**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:** 
A sweeping, moody drone shot of a crashing ocean wave against a jagged, isolated cliff, transitioning to an archival photo of the Eilean Mor lighthouse.

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a relief ship arrived at the remote Flannan Isles off the coast of Scotland. What the crew found inside the lighthouse defied logic. All three keepers had vanished without a trace. 

The heavy entrance door was bolted from the inside. The clocks had stopped. Half-eaten meals sat on the kitchen table, and the logbook recorded a violent storm—yet no storms had been reported in the area. The island was completely deserted, and no bodies were ever recovered. To this day, the final fate of the men remains officially unexplained.

**Visual Suggestion 2:** 
A close-up shot of an old, weathered leather journal opening, flipping to handwritten pages under dim, flickering lamp light.

**Narration (Cont.):**
Official investigations suggested the men were swept away by a rogue wave while securing equipment near the cliffs. However, speculation runs wild. Some suggest a sudden, fatal madness; others whisper of foreign abduction or sea monsters.

### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In December 1900, keepers on Eilean Mor (Flannan Isles) disappeared.
* The relief ship *Hesperus* arrived to find the station unattended.
* The entrance gate and door were closed and bolted, clocks were stopped, and meals were left out.
* The final log entries noted severe, unusual weather, though local meteorological reports disputed a storm of that magnitude at that exact time.
* No bodies or signs of the men were ever found.

**Speculation:**
* The official theory suggests a massive rogue wave washed the men away while they were working near the shore. 
* Unsubstantiated theories include industrial accidents, psychological breakdowns, foreign spies, or supernatural phenomena.
