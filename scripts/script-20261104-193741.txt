### **Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown**

*   **Verified Facts:** 
    *   In December 1900, the supply ship *Hesperus* arrived at the Eilean Mor lighthouse in the Flannan Isles and found it unmanned.
    *   The station was locked from the inside, the kitchen fire was cold, a meal was left half-eaten, and the logbook contained strange entries regarding severe weather. 
    *   Keepers James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur vanished without a trace, and their bodies were never recovered.
    *   Official weather records for the area indicated calm conditions on the days leading up to their disappearance.

*   **Speculation:** 
    *   The exact cause of their disappearance (e.g., whether they were swept away by an unexpected rogue wave while securing equipment, or if human conflict/madness played a role) cannot be proven due to a complete lack of physical evidence or witnesses.
