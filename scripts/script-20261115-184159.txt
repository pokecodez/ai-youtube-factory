### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
*   Joe Labelle was a fur trapper who operated in the remote regions of northern Canada in 1930.
*   He reported visiting an Inuit fishing village near Lake Anjikuni where he found the inhabitants missing and personal belongings left behind.
*   The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) investigated the report, noting the presence of supplies and unattended fires. 

**Speculation / Folklore:**
*   The claim that seven sled dogs were found starved to death under deep snow while food was nearby originates largely from sensationalized newspaper accounts and later ufologist retellings (most notably author Frank Edwards in his 1959 book *Stranger Than Science*). 
*   Official RCMP reports contemporary to the event found little evidence to support the more supernatural or mass-mysterious elements popularized in later decades, and subsequent investigations suggested the village may not have been permanently inhabited at the time Labelle arrived, as transient Inuit groups frequently moved camps based on seasonal hunting conditions.
