**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:**

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a routine supply ship arrived at the remote Flannan Isles lighthouse off the coast of Scotland. What the crew found inside sent chills through the British Empire. The heavy iron entrance gate was bolted shut. Inside, the kitchen fire was stone cold, untouched meals sat on a table, and two of the three waterproof oilskins were missing. But the most terrifying detail? The clock on the wall had stopped ticking, and the station’s logbook recorded entries of terrifying gale-force winds—yet meteorological records showed the weather that week had been completely calm. The three keepers vanished without a trace, leaving behind an island shrouded in eternal silence. Was it a freak rogue wave that swept them away while inspecting equipment, or something far more baffling? To this day, the fate of the Flannan Isles keepers remains unsolved.

### **Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown**

*   **Verified Facts:**
    *   In December 1900, the supply vessel *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse and found it completely deserted.
    *   The station's entrance gate was secured, the kitchen fire was unlit, meals were left untouched, and two of the three heavy waterproof coats were missing.
    *   The lighthouse logbook contained frantic entries about severe gales and high winds in the days leading up to the disappearance.
    *   The three keepers (James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur) were never found.

*   **Speculation:**
    *   **The Cause of Disappearance:** Official inquiry reports speculated that a sudden, massive rogue wave may have swept one or more of the men off the rocks while they were securing equipment near the shore. 
    *   **The Weather Discrepancy:** The discrepancy between the logbook (describing severe gales) and mainland meteorological records (showing calm weather) is debated; some historians suggest it points to panic or human error in record-keeping, while popular folklore attributes it to supernatural forces.
