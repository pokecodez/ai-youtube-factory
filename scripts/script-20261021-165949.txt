**Narration Script:**
In October 1955, the merchant vessel *Joyita* set sail from Samoa with twenty-five souls on board. Five weeks later, she was found drifting half-submerged in the Pacific. 

The ship was severely damaged, her radio was tuned to the international distress frequency, and her logs and navigation equipment were gone. But most chilling of all? Not a single passenger or crew member was ever found.

Theories range from a sudden pirate attack to the captain being washed overboard, sparking a panic. To this day, the fate of the *Joyita*’s crew remains one of the Pacific’s most baffling maritime mysteries.

### **Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown**

*   **Verified Facts:**
    *   The *Joyita* was a merchant vessel that was found abandoned and half-submerged in the South Pacific in November 1955 (having departed in October).
    *   There were 25 people on board (crew and passengers), and none of them were ever found.
    *   The ship's cargo, logs, and some equipment were missing, and the radio was found tuned to the distress channel.
    *   Official maritime inquiries confirmed the ship was buoyant and practically unsinkable due to cork-lined holds.

*   **Speculation:**
    *   The exact reason *why* the crew abandoned the ship remains unknown. 
    *   Popular theories include a sudden medical emergency of the captain followed by panic, a Japanese fishing boat encounter, or insurance fraud, but none of these have definitive evidence.
