**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a supply ship reached the remote Flannan Isles lighthouse off the coast of Scotland, only to find something impossible. The island was completely deserted. 

Inside the lighthouse, the clock had stopped. The kitchen fire was cold, and two of the three waterproof coats were missing—meaning someone had gone out into a violent storm without proper gear. 

The heavy iron entrance gate was bolted shut. The logbook detailed terrifying gales that never actually happened in the meteorological records of the mainland.

###
 Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
*   In December 1900, the relief vessel *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse and found it unoccupied. 
*   The station’s logbook contained entries detailing severe gales prior to the disappearance, though mainland reports noted calm weather during those dates.
*   Two of the three keepers' oilskins (waterproof coats) were missing, but the third man's coat was left behind.
*   The interior of the lighthouse was orderly, though a kitchen chair was overturned.
*   An official government investigation concluded that the men were likely swept away by unexpected, massive rogue waves while securing equipment near the landing stage.

**Speculation:**
*   Popular folklore and ghost stories have suggested alien abduction, sea monsters, foreign spies, or a violent madness overcoming the men. 
*   Theories involving a sudden rogue wave washing the men away are considered the most plausible expert speculation, but because no bodies were ever recovered, the exact sequence of events remains officially unknown.
