**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:** 
A moody, cinematic drone shot crashing over dark, jagged Scottish cliffs at dusk, slowly zooming in on a solitary stone lighthouse against a grey sky.

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a ship passing Scotland’s remote Flannan Isles noticed something chilling: the lighthouse lamp was unlit. When relief keeper Joseph Moore finally arrived on the island days later, he found the station completely deserted. The heavy iron entrance gate was locked, two of the three oilskins were left behind, and inside, the kitchen table was set with uneaten food. The station’s clock had mysteriously stopped. Log entries detailed terrifying gale-force winds, yet local weather stations reported calm seas during that exact window. Despite an exhaustive search, the three experienced keepers—James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur—were never seen again. What caused three seasoned men to abandon their post into a freezing winter night?

### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In December 1900, the Eilean Mor lighthouse on the Flannan Isles was found unlit.
* Relief keeper Joseph Moore discovered the station abandoned upon his arrival.
* The entrance gate was shut, the kitchen table had food on it, and two of the three keepers' waterproof coats were left inside.
* The three keepers (Ducat, Marshall, and MacArthur) disappeared and were never found.
* Official log entries in the days prior mentioned severe winds, though mainland weather reports noted calm conditions in the immediate area.

**Speculation:**
* Theories suggesting the men were swept away by a rogue wave while securing equipment.
* Folkloric or supernatural speculations regarding sea monsters, ghost ships, or foreign abduction (all lacking any physical evidence).
* Theories of a sudden argument or madness driving the men into the sea.
