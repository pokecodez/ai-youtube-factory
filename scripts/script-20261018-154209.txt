In December 1900, the supply ship *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse off the coast of Scotland, expecting a warm welcome. Instead, the keepers were gone. 

Inside the lighthouse, the fire was out, the kitchen clock had stopped, and two of the three heavy waterproof coats were missing—suggesting only one man had gone outside. Yet, standard safety protocol strictly required at least two men to leave the station during a storm.

Despite an exhaustive search, no bodies were ever recovered. What drove three experienced men to vanish into the Atlantic, leaving their post completely abandoned? The true fate of the Flannan Isles keepers remains an official, impenetrable mystery.
