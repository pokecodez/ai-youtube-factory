### Historical Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In 1587, John White led 115 English colonists to Roanoke Island.
* Due to the Anglo-Spanish War, White’s return to the island with supplies was delayed until 1590.
* Upon his return, the settlement was empty, and no colonists were found.
* The word "CROATAN" was carved into a post, and "CRO" was carved into a tree. No crosses (the pre-arranged distress signal) were present.
* Croatoan was the name of a nearby island and the Native American tribe living there.

**Speculation:**
* Exactly *why* the colonists left the settlement without leaving a distress signal remains unknown.
* Whether they were completely assimilated by the Croatoan people, attacked by other tribes, lost at sea while attempting to sail home, or died of starvation/disease is still debated by historians and archaeologists, as no definitive consensus or complete physical evidence has been found.
