**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:**

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a routine supply ship arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse off the coast of Scotland, only to find something impossible. Total emptiness. 

The heavy entrance door was bolted from the inside. Inside, the kitchen fire was unlit, the clocks had stopped, and two of the three keepers' oilskin coats were missing. Worst of all, the station’s logbook recorded gale-force winds that never actually happened. 

What drove three experienced men into the freezing Atlantic without their warm gear? Was it a freak rogue wave that swept them away while checking equipment? Or the crushing isolation of the harsh Scottish winter? 

More than a century later, the fate of the Flannan Isles keepers remains unsolved.

### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In December 1900, the supply steamer *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse and found the station deserted.
* The entrance door was bolted from the inside, the kitchen fire was cold, and clocks had stopped.
* Two of the three keepers' waterproof coats were missing, leaving only one behind.
* The lighthouse logbook contained unusual entries regarding severe weather that meteorological records show did not occur in that exact area at that time.
* Despite extensive searches, no bodies were ever recovered.

**Speculation:**
* Theories suggesting a rogue wave swept the men away while they were securing equipment near the cliffs.
* Theories involving psychological stress, madness, or foul play among the keepers.
* Any definitive explanation for why the men left without all of their cold-weather gear.
