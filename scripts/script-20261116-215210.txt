**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:** 
A moody, cinematic drone shot of the remote, wave-battered Flannan Isles off the coast of Scotland, fading to vintage sepia photographs of the three missing lighthouse keepers.

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a supply ship arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse in Scotland, only to find something impossible. The island was completely deserted. 

Inside the lighthouse, the clock had stopped. The kitchen fire was unlit, and two of the three waterproof coats were missing—meaning someone had left in a hurry. Yet, the logbook recorded terrifying details from the previous days: hurricane-force winds that shook the station, and frantic weeping from normally stoic men. 

Their bodies were never found. To this day, no one knows what drove three experienced keepers into the freezing Atlantic.

### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In December 1900, the supply vessel *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse to find it unmanned.
* The station's logbook contained entries detailing severe gales and distress in the days leading up to the disappearance.
* Two of the three oilskins (waterproof coats) were gone, but one was left behind, suggesting one man went out without his coat, or all coats weren't needed.
* Extensive searches by authorities yielded no trace of the keepers—James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur.

**Speculation:**
* The cause of the disappearance (whether they were swept away by a freak rogue wave while securing equipment, or if panic/madness played a role). 
* The exact emotional state of the men, as logs can be misinterpreted or sensationalized over time.
