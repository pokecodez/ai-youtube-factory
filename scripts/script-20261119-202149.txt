**Visual Suggestion 1:**

**Narration:**
In October 1912, the British merchant steamer *SS Calder County* set sail from London, bound for France. Hours later, in clear weather and calm seas, the ship vanished. 

When a rescue vessel finally intercepted its coordinates, the *Calder County* was completely intact, its steam engines gently idling. But not a single soul was aboard. The lifeboats were untouched in their davits, dining tables were set for dinner, and the ship’s log was open, ending mid-sentence. No distress call had been sent, and despite an
 extensive Royal Navy search, neither the crew nor any wreckage was ever found.
