In December 1900, a supply ship arrived at the remote Flannan Isles off the coast of Scotland. The lighthouse sat dark and silent, stripped of its guiding light. Inside, the three-man crew—James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur—had vanished entirely.

### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In December 1900, the supply vessel *Hesperus* arrived at the Eilean Mor lighthouse in the Flannan Isles and found it unmanned.
* The three resident keepers (Ducat, Marshall, and MacArthur) were missing.
* The station's logbook contained entries regarding a severe storm, though official mainland weather logs recorded calm conditions in the area at that time.
* The kitchen table showed evidence of a meal interrupted, and two of the keepers' waterproof jackets were missing, while a third was left behind.
* No trace of the men was ever found.

**Speculation:**
* Theories suggesting the men were swept away by a sudden, massive rogue wave while securing equipment near the cliffs (the leading official theory, but unproven due to lack of eyewitnesses).
* Folkloric and sensationalized speculations involving foreign spies, phantom ships, sea monsters, or madness induced by isolation.
