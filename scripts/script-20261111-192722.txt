**Narration:**
In November 1923, the British cargo ship *SS Marion* entered the notoriously treacherous Dover Strait—and simply vanished. There was no distress call. No wreckage was ever found. But what makes this case deeply unsettling is what happened twelve hours later. The *Marion’s* exact logbook washed ashore inside a sealed, watertight canister, perfectly dry, on a beach fifty miles away. Inside, the final entry was scrawled in the captain’s distinct handwriting, reading only: "The fog smells like copper. We are not
 alone." The ship was never located, and the origin of the drifting logbook remains completely unexplained. What pulled the *SS Marion* into the abyss?
