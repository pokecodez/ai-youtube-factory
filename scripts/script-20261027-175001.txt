### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
*   In December 1900, the relief ship *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse and found it completely deserted.
*   The three keepers (Ducat, Marshall, and MacArthur) were missing.
*   The station logbook contained unusual entries regarding extreme, localized weather conditions leading up to their disappearance.
*   Equipment, including a supply crane, showed signs of heavy storm damage.
*   Extensive searches yielded no bodies or definitive clues to their fate.

**Speculation (Theories without definitive evidence):**
*   That the men were swept away by a rogue wave while trying to secure equipment near the shore.
*   Pioneering sensationalist theories of the era, such as alien abduction, foreign spies, or a madness-induced murder-suicide among the keepers.
