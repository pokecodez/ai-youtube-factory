### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
*   On December 26, 1900, the steamer *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse and found it unmanned. 
*   Keepers James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur were missing.
*   The station's logbook contained unusual weather reports for days when neighboring areas experienced calm conditions.
*   Only one set of waterproof oilskins remained behind, suggesting one man went out without proper gear.

**Speculation:**
*   Popular theories suggest a sudden, rogue wave swept MacArthur away while he tried to secure equipment, and his two colleagues perished attempting a rescue. 
*   More sensational, unproven rumors involve foreign spies, phantom ships, or mental breakdown due to isolation.
