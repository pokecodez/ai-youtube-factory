**Title:** The Vanishing of the Dover Strait Phantom

**Visual Suggestion 1:** 
A moody, cinematic drone shot panning over turbulent, grey waves in the English Channel at twilight, shifting to a grainy, black-and-white 1920s newspaper clipping with the headline: "PILOT VANISHES INTO THIN AIR."

**Narration:**
In December 1920, veteran British pilot Raymond Collishaw peered into a dense fog bank over the English Channel. Minutes later, flying over the Dover Strait, aviator Stewart Muir Johnstone watched in absolute disbelief as a fellow aircraft simply dissolved into the white mist. No distress call. No engine trouble. Just sudden, total disappearance.

**Visual Suggestion 2:**
Archival footage of a biplane flying through clouds, transitioning to a split-screen graphic: one side labeled "VERIFIED FACTS" showing flight logs, and the other side labeled "SPECULATION" showing a question mark over a swirling vortex graphic.

**Narration:**
The verified facts are chillingly brief: a pilot and his plane vanished mid-flight with zero wreckage ever recovered from the sea. Speculation quickly ran wild, blaming sudden air pockets, spatial disorientation in the fog, or even early anomalies of the infamous Bermuda Triangle. To this day, the true fate of the Dover Strait flight remains an unsolved aviation ghost story.
