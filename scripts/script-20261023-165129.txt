**Title:** The Vanishing of Lighthouse Island

**Visual Suggestion 1:** 
A sweeping, moody drone shot of a grey, crashing ocean surrounding a desolate, rocky cliff topped by a solitary Victorian lighthouse. 

**Narration:**
In December 1900, a supply ship arrived at the remote Flannan Isles lighthouse off the coast of Scotland. It found a chilling scene. The heavy iron gate was locked. Inside, the kitchen fire was cold, and two oilskins were missing, but the third man’s coat was left behind. The clocks had stopped. Most eerie of all, the station log recorded terrifying hurricane-force winds—yet meteorological records showed the weather that week had been completely calm. The three experienced keepers—James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur—had simply vanished without a trace, leaving behind a mystery that remains unsolved over a century later.

### Fact vs. Speculation Breakdown

**Verified Facts:**
* In December 1900, the supply vessel *Hesperus* arrived at the Flannan Isles lighthouse and found the station unmanned.
* The station's gate was locked, the kitchen fire was unlit, two of the three keepers' waterproof coats were gone, and one was left behind.
* The station log contained entries describing severe, hurricane-force winds during the days leading up to the discovery.

**Speculation (Theories without definitive evidence):**
* Meteorological records from mainland stations and nearby ships at the time did not report any major storms in the area, leading to debate over why the log mentioned extreme weather.
* Popular speculation over the decades has included rogue waves washing the men away, accidents during crane maintenance on the cliffs, foreign abduction, or psychological breakdown—none of which have physical evidence to confirm them.
